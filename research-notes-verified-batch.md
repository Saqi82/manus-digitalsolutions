# Verified Software Content Research Notes

## Xero UK

**Source:** [Xero UK official website](https://www.xero.com/uk/), accessed 21 August 2026.

Xero’s UK page presents the product as bringing invoicing, VAT, payroll and reporting together. It links to features for invoicing, accepting payments, inventory management, payroll, expenses and Making Tax Digital. It describes itself as an HMRC-recognised software provider for MTD. The page also presents plans and time-limited introductory prices; these values are not reproduced in proposed evergreen article copy because pricing and promotions are time-sensitive.

## QuickBooks UK

**Source:** [QuickBooks UK official website](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/uk/), accessed 21 August 2026.

QuickBooks UK describes invoicing and online payments, expense tracking, a customisable dashboard, reports, budgets on certain plans, inventory on certain plans, cash-flow reporting, Making Tax Digital readiness, payroll-related workflows and bank-account connections. Feature availability varies by plan, and article copy should direct readers to current official pricing and plan information before deciding.

## Editorial use

The new articles will avoid star ratings, product prices, unverified ease-of-use claims and definitive “best” conclusions. They will use an evidence-led framework: stated product scope, possible fit, verification questions, UK considerations and links to the relevant official sources.

## FreeAgent

**Source:** [FreeAgent official website](https://www.freeagent.com/), accessed 21 August 2026.

FreeAgent positions the product for small businesses, freelancers and contractors, landlords, and accountants or bookkeepers. Its official page describes invoicing, cash-flow support, tax workflows, payroll, payments and Making Tax Digital. It states that its MTD solution supports VAT and Self Assessment submissions to HMRC, and identifies its product as HMRC-recognised. Pricing and account-linked offers are time-sensitive and will not be reproduced as evergreen claims.

## Google Workspace UK

**Source:** [Google Workspace UK official website](https://workspace.google.com/intl/en_uk/), accessed 21 August 2026.

Google Workspace states that its plans include custom business email and collaboration applications including Gmail, Calendar, Meet, Chat, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms and Sites. It also links to Google’s business administration, AI and security materials. Product plan scope and price should be checked on Google’s current plan page before a purchase decision.

## Microsoft 365 Business UK

**Source:** [Microsoft 365 Business UK official website](https://www.microsoft.com/en-gb/microsoft-365/business), accessed 21 August 2026.

Microsoft’s UK business page presents Microsoft 365 as a work suite covering Microsoft productivity applications, custom business email, cloud storage, Teams communication and additional applications such as Bookings, Planner and Forms. Its current plan listings show that available applications and security capabilities vary by plan. Pricing and promotional terms will be linked to the current official page rather than stated as evergreen article facts.

## Content batch selected

The implemented batch will consist of five source-led routes: **Xero review**, **QuickBooks review**, **Xero vs QuickBooks UK**, **FreeAgent vs Xero UK**, and **Google Workspace vs Microsoft 365 UK**. Each page will link its factual product-scope statements to the appropriate official source and retain a clear reader prompt to verify current plan details.
