# High-Intent Keyword Expansion: Research and Prioritization

**Purpose.** This expansion adds useful, commercially relevant software-category content only where it strengthens DigitalSolutions.cv’s existing subject focus: business software, operations, customer work, finance routines, people operations, communications, security, growth, commerce, and productivity.

The opportunity audit considered **187 candidate phrases**. It excluded **46 routes already covered** by the site and retained **141 unique keyword opportunities**. The retained set is grouped into the site’s existing authority clusters so that each new page has a clear parent topic, reciprocal routes, and a relevant audience decision rather than standing as an isolated keyword page.

| Priority topic | New pages | Reader decision the pages support |
|---|---:|---|
| Operations, ERP and industry workflows | 20 | Whether an operating system can make planning, inventory, delivery, records, and exceptions more visible. |
| Customer service and support | 16 | How to manage requests, ownership, feedback, help-desk work, and customer follow-through. |
| Finance and invoicing workflows | 13 | How to assess expense, cash-flow, planning, billing, and approval routines without giving financial or tax advice. |
| People operations | 14 | How to make onboarding, workforce records, time, development, and hiring hand-offs clear. |
| Communication utilities | 15 | How teams coordinate calls, messages, meetings, shared inboxes, files, and remote support. |
| Security and storage | 18 | How to evaluate access, resilience, backup, endpoint, and information-protection workflows. |
| Marketing and web | 17 | How to turn audience signals into deliberate campaigns, lead capture, web pages, and measurement. |
| E-commerce systems | 12 | How to connect catalogue, order, fulfilment, subscription, and commerce hand-offs. |
| Productivity and collaboration | 16 | How to make projects, capacity, planning, decisions, and shared work easier to run. |

## Prioritization approach

This work treats terms such as **ERP software**, **help desk software**, **expense tracking software**, **HRIS software**, **password manager**, **marketing automation software**, and **project portfolio management software** as high-intent commercial topics: a reader can reasonably be expected to be evaluating a system, category, or workflow. The expansion does **not** claim exact monthly search volume, because no authenticated Keyword Planner or first-party Search Console data was supplied.

Google describes Trends as a way to compare relative interest, discover related and rising queries, and ensure a topic fits the site and its users.[1] It also advises creators to focus on people-first content that has a clear audience and substantially helps the reader accomplish a goal.[2] The pages therefore provide a workflow-specific question, decision prompt, signals, checkpoints, parent-pillar link, and reciprocal topic network rather than a thin list of tools.

> **Editorial guardrail.** This is an opportunity-led content expansion, not a claim that every phrase has a verified numerical search volume. Search Console and Google Trends should be used after publication to rank and refine the pages based on actual audience demand.[1]
