# Business Software Homepage GEO and AEO Update

## Scope

The homepage targets **business software**. The implementation is designed to answer the category-level intent directly while routing visitors to the site’s distinct hubs for business software, automation, AI tools, calculators, guides, reviews, and comparisons.

## Reference observations

The reviewed competitor, [Business-Software.com](https://www.business-software.com/), organises the category around broad software families including accounting, ERP, CRM, HR, business intelligence, research reports, and product-oriented discovery. DigitalSolutions.cv differentiates by foregrounding a transparent workflow-first decision process rather than lead-generation claims, rankings, or undocumented product assertions.

The homepage includes one cited contextual adoption signal from the [U.S. Chamber of Commerce 2025 Empowering Small Business report](https://www.uschamber.com/technology/empowering-small-business-the-impact-of-technology-on-u-s-small-business). The source reports that 84% of surveyed small businesses planned to increase their use of technology platforms. This number is presented as context for deliberate selection, not as a performance claim or a reason to purchase software.

## Implementation decisions

The page now begins its post-hero content with a 40–60-word bolded direct answer to “What is business software?” It uses a structured decision canvas, entity-focused business-software explanations, a four-step selection method, contextual links to CRM, accounting, project management, business automation, and AI tools, and five conversational FAQs.

FAQPage structured data is emitted only on the homepage using the visible FAQ copy. The visual system adds a decision-canvas graphic alongside the existing hero, software, and calculator graphics, with descriptive alt text for the primary software visual.
