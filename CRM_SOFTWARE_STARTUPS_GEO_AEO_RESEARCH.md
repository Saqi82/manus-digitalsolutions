# CRM Software for Startups GEO and AEO Update

## Search intent

**CRM software for startups** is a commercial research query. A useful page should help an early-stage team decide what customer process needs structure now, which customer records and ownership cues are essential, and what limits should be checked before the process becomes more complex.

## Verified provider references

The page will use neutral examples rather than a universal ranking. Current official pricing and plan pages provide the following time-sensitive reference points:

| Provider | Current reference point | Source |
|---|---|---|
| HubSpot Sales Hub | Free tier for up to two users; Starter displayed from $7 per seat per month when billed annually. | [HubSpot Sales Hub pricing](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing/sales) |
| Pipedrive | Lite displayed at $14 per seat per month when billed annually; 14-day trial stated on the pricing page. | [Pipedrive pricing](https://www.pipedrive.com/en/pricing) |
| Zoho CRM | Free edition supports up to three users; country and currency selection affect displayed paid pricing. | [Zoho CRM pricing](https://www.zoho.com/en-us/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html) |

Pricing, currencies, scope, limits, support, privacy and integration availability can change. The page will link directly to the official provider pricing sources and prompt readers to check the current terms before relying on an entry-level plan.

## Content and visual plan

The update will add a 45–60 word direct answer, provider reference table, a split-window startup customer-motion instrument, stage-based implementation guidance, five conversational FAQs, FAQPage structured data, and links to CRM software, software comparisons, email marketing software, business automation and startup operations. The page will use four native interface-style visual assets with descriptive accessible labels rather than unverified third-party dashboard imagery.
