# Chosen Direction — The Editorial Terminal

## Design Movement

Contemporary editorial systems fused with high-quality SaaS product design. The experience draws from a well-made UK technology publication rather than a generic blog or agency template.

## Core Principles

1. **Information leads:** Every surface gives a reader a next useful decision, category or tool.
2. **Editorial precision:** Generous typography, clear labels and strong hierarchy make complex software research feel approachable.
3. **Purposeful contrast:** Deep navy anchors navigation and key moments; paper-white content areas keep reading calm and clear.
4. **Structured variation:** Cards share a system but vary in scale and orientation to prevent repetitive dashboard-like layouts.

## Colour Philosophy

Navy provides authority, Charcoal provides readability, near-white paper surfaces create calm, and a precise professional blue identifies action and interactive states. A quiet pale blue supports information grouping without introducing decoration for its own sake.

## Layout Paradigm

The homepage is an editorial route rather than a centred stack: a left-led hero narrative meets a visual product panel, followed by an off-axis category rail, alternating full-bleed information bands and asymmetric collections. Reading paths are continuous rather than grid-first.

## Signature Elements

1. **Signal tags:** compact uppercase category labels and meta markers that make content status clear.
2. **Interface specimens:** bespoke software, AI and calculator panels which function as visual context, not stock photography.
3. **Fine rule system:** understated vertical rails, dividers and index-like numbering that hold long pages together.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should provide calm confirmation: links shift with a small directional motion, cards reveal a concise action cue and the mobile menu uses a clear, non-blocking overlay. Controls are fully keyboard reachable with distinctly visible focus styles.

## Animation

Use 160–220ms transform and opacity transitions with a sharp ease-out curve. Card and button states move by no more than 3–4px. Hero interface pieces may float subtly only when reduced motion is not requested; all non-essential motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography System

**Manrope** is used for the utilitarian sans-serif body, labels and navigation. **DM Serif Display** is used selectively for long-form editorial emphasis and high-value section titles. Headlines use strong weight contrast, sentence case and compact tracking; labels use small uppercase letterspaced text.

## Brand Essence

**A clear, practical UK decision platform for people selecting business technology, software and everyday digital tools.**

**Personality:** precise, grounded, resourceful.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are direct and task-led; CTAs name the action and microcopy explains the benefit without hype.

Example lines: “Choose software with the right jobs in mind.”  
“Run the numbers before you commit.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a bold, abstract split-window symbol: two offset navy tiles divided by a blue signal line, suggesting both a browser window and a considered comparison. The wordmark pairs the symbol with a custom-spaced DigitalSolutions.cv lock-up rather than a default text treatment.

## Signature Brand Colour

**Signal Blue — #1E6BFF**

## Style Decisions

- Every route opening uses at least three Editorial Terminal signatures: an uppercase signal label, an index or fine-rule device, and a navy or pale-paper decision panel.
- Calculator routes are treated as working instruments: input, result and assumptions are bound by ledger-like dividers, compact data labels and Signal Blue reserved for actions or computed priorities.
- Inner-page hierarchy combines Manrope weight contrast with selective DM Serif phrasing rather than relying only on oversized sans-serif titles.
- The split-window mark informs divided panels, offset technical tiles and precise blue signal lines beyond the logo itself.
- Reviews and Comparisons use distinct collection rhythms: reviews emphasise source checking and product-fit prompts, while comparisons present visible A/B workflow panels and side-by-side decision framing.
- Major editorial routes include a visible decision specimen or split-window evidence panel, ensuring the technology-publication character does not depend on the homepage alone.
- Evidence panels should expose a practical workflow, assumption, evidence, and outcome zone so they read as usable decision instruments rather than decorative cards.
- Reserve DM Serif Display for major editorial emphasis; let repeated hierarchy come from Manrope weight, numbered structures, fine rules, and navy/paper contrast.
