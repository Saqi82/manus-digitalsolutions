# Best Free CRM Software: GEO, AEO and SEO Research Notes

## Page focus

The target page is `/best-free-crm-software/`, the next CRM keyword landing page after `/crm-software-comparison/` in the current sitemap sequence. Its primary keyword is **best free CRM software**.

## Search intent and page approach

The page should clarify that a "best" free CRM is conditional on the customer workflow, team maintenance capacity and free-plan scope that can be verified today. It should avoid presenting an invented universal ranking. The reader should instead compare free options against one real routine, the included user and feature boundaries, the operating effort, and the threshold that will trigger a plan review.

## Official provider references checked

## Content and visual plan

The direct answer will appear immediately after the H1. The expanded content will use a free-plan reference table, a "free-plan boundary" visual, a suitability instrument, implementation checks, contextual internal links, and visible conversational FAQs with matching FAQPage structured data. The content will link naturally to `/free-crm-for-small-business/`, `/crm-software-comparison/`, `/crm-software-for-startups/`, and `/business-automation/` while retaining the CRM cluster's reciprocal links.
