# DigitalSolutions.cv Topical Authority Architecture

DigitalSolutions.cv now organises its keyword library around eleven **people-first decision pillars**. Each pillar connects a bounded business task, its supporting software and utility clusters, practical guidance, and evidence-led comparisons. This strengthens topic discovery without presenting mass-generated pages as generic recommendations.

| Authority principle | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Clear site focus | Ten visible pillars make the site’s primary purpose—practical business software, tools and decision guidance—easy to navigate. |
| Descriptive, crawlable paths | Links use real route paths, clear anchor text and contextual labels. |
| Hub-to-supporting-page discovery | The authority map exposes curated pillar routes; every keyword page receives a link back to its pillar pathway. |
| People-first usefulness | The model starts with a business decision and repeated workflow, then offers related pages, tools and comparisons as next steps. |
| Transparent comparisons | Named product examples are review shortlists, not ratings or customer-review claims. Readers are prompted to verify current provider information directly. |

The model responds to the current coverage audit: **854 keyword pages across 45 topic clusters**, with the deepest existing hubs in Business Software, Free Tools, Business Guides and AI Tools. The authority map adds structured entry points so visitors do not need to discover these topics only through search or six-page reciprocal clusters.

## Research basis

Google describes links as signals for page relevance and discovery, recommends descriptive anchor text, and advises that pages a site cares about should be linked from other relevant pages.[1] Google also recommends helpful, reliable, people-first content with a primary focus and clear information about how content is created.[2]
