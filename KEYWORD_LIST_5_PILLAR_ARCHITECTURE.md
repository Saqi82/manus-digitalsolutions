## Internal linking requirement

Every cluster has at least eight selected pages. The shared page renderer creates a **six-link reciprocal network** inside the same cluster, and each page also includes a parent-hub destination and a topical-authority Pillar Pathway. This gives a reader multiple routes to adjacent questions without treating the website as a disconnected set of keyword targets.

## Editorial and safety approach

The pages use a small-business lens: an operating outcome, current routine, accountable owner, useful evidence, and a bounded next step. They do not make unverified price, feature, compliance, or security claims. Security content is educational and encourages an appropriate professional review for material decisions.

CISA recommends a role-based cybersecurity action plan for small businesses, emphasizes that security is a leadership and organisational responsibility, and calls out practical activities including multi-factor authentication, incident-response planning, training, and tabletop exercises.[1] FTC small-business guidance includes cybersecurity basics, secure remote access, email authentication, vendor security, the NIST framework, and data-breach response resources.[2] These sources inform the practical, non-alarmist framing used on the security pages.

> **Editorial guardrail.** Pages about cybersecurity, finance, legal compliance, or software implementation help readers structure a decision; they do not replace a qualified review of an organisation’s specific obligations, systems, or risks.
