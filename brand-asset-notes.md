# DigitalSolutions.cv Brand Asset Findings

The previously generated symbol-mark files are 1920 × 1920 PNGs containing the established split-window logo motif: navy modular panels, a decisive blue diagonal signal and three small pale status dots. Both available PNGs include an intense green background, so they are unsuitable as direct header or favicon assets.

The production assets will retain the split-window concept but use a clean navy-and-signal-blue treatment on a transparent or white-safe background. This will preserve the existing Editorial Terminal identity while delivering reliable contrast at favicon scale and in the website header.
